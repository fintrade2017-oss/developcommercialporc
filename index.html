4. Modelo Económico de Comisiones · 20% por Cierre 4. Commission Economic Model · 20% per Closing

Estructura única · Sin escalones · Pagos automatizados Single structure · No tiers · Automated payouts

Cada Membresía Executive cerrada = $50.000 USD netos al intermediario Each closed Executive Membership = $50,000 USD net to the intermediary

$250.000 × 20% = $50.000 USD

La comisión del 20% se calcula sobre el valor nominal de la Membresía Executive ($250.000 USD) y se libera de forma automática y simultánea al desembolso del 10% inicial del cliente al fideicomiso panameño (Ley 1/84). El pago se efectúa a una cuenta segregada a nombre del intermediario, designada previamente en el Sale & Purchase Agreement. No hay período de retención ni cláusulas de clawback: la comisión se gana al cierre y se paga al cierre. The 20% commission is calculated on the face value of the Executive Membership ($250,000 USD) and is released automatically and simultaneously upon the client's 10% initial deposit into the Panamanian trust (Law 1/84). Payment is made to a segregated account in the intermediary's name, previously designated in the Sale & Purchase Agreement. There is no retention period or clawback clause: the commission is earned at closing and paid at closing.

Sin apalancamiento bancario adicional. The total cost of the commercial operation (personnel + OPEX + commissions) is fully covered by the free fiduciary surplus ($1,500M USD) generated by the difference between the net capital inflow ($2,250M) and the consolidated CAPEX ($750M). No additional bank leverage.
